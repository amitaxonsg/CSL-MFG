# Export positioning and discovery — version 1.2

## Content and navigation
Design Gallery replaces Jersey Gallery; /gallery redirects permanently to /design-gallery. Main routes include Industries, Design Gallery, International Buyers, Products, Capabilities, Fabrics, RFQ and Contact. Prominent top utility buttons replace the small labels.

Buyer groups, in order:
1. Sports & Teams — 14 sports, rugby featured.
2. Hospitality — hotels/resorts, restaurants/cafés, bars/entertainment.
3. Corporate & Retail — companies/offices, retail/private label.
4. Industry & Workwear — factories, logistics/transport, trades/service teams.
5. Education & Community — schools/universities, events/exhibitions, community/charity.

There are 25 collections with 12 illustrative designs each (300 concepts). A generated industry concept board supplies a photographic design direction; original SVG concepts remain illustrative. Ordinary workwear categories do not claim protective clothing or certifications. Product suitability must be confirmed.

Export buyers are primary; Philippine customers remain welcome. The export guide covers briefs, artwork, production approvals, shipping responsibilities and documentation, without invented duty exemptions, prices or delivery promises.

## Technical discovery
SSR content; unique page titles and descriptions; canonical URLs; Organization, WebSite, WebPage and BreadcrumbList structured data; descriptive headings and image alternatives; sitemap with original-image references; expanded llms.txt with facts, public links, location and honest Phase 2 feature status. Font files are locally hosted Manrope under OFL, with no Google request during visits.

SEARCH_INDEXING remains false during review and unfinished email activation. It must be deliberately enabled at launch. Sitemap presence does not override noindex or robots blocking. No ranking or AI-citation guarantee is made. llms.txt is supplementary context, not a Google ranking requirement.
